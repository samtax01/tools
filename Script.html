Popular Hotels to Stay in New York

Discover the best hotels in New York for a comfortable and memorable stay. Browse through our top picks below.

The Fifth Avenue Hotel – A Gilded Age Fantasy Reimagined

Step into a world where history meets modern luxury at The Fifth Avenue Hotel. Nestled near Madison Square Park, this hotel occupies a restored 19th-century mansion complemented by a contemporary glass tower. Each of its 153 rooms and suites exudes opulence, featuring lavish marble bathrooms and curated art collections.

Café Carmellini, the hotel's Italian restaurant, has quickly become a culinary hotspot, offering exquisite dishes in a theatrical setting. Whether you're sipping cocktails at The Portrait Bar or exploring nearby NoMad attractions, The Fifth Avenue Hotel offers a blend of timeless elegance and modern flair.

The Wall Street Hotel – Nautical Elegance in the Financial District

Experience refined luxury at The Wall Street Hotel, situated in the heart of Manhattan's Financial District. Owned by the Paspaley family, renowned for their pearling legacy, the hotel infuses maritime elements throughout its design—from oyster-inspired watercolors to mother-of-pearl accents.

Rooms feature plush velvet furnishings, deep soaking tubs, and heated bathroom floors, ensuring a comfortable stay. Dine at La Marchande, the in-house brasserie led by Michelin-starred chef John Fraser, offering a menu rich in seafood delicacies. With its blend of historical charm and modern amenities, The Wall Street Hotel provides a serene retreat amidst the city's hustle.

Aman New York – A Sanctuary of Tranquility in Midtown Manhattan

Nestled within the historic Crown Building on Fifth Avenue, Aman New York redefines urban luxury. This serene retreat offers 83 expansive suites, each adorned with Japanese-inspired design elements, gas fireplaces, and spa-like bathrooms. Guests can indulge in holistic wellness at the hotel's extensive spa facilities, including an indoor pool and treatment rooms. Dining options feature world-class cuisine, providing a culinary journey without leaving the premises.

Aman New York's central location places guests steps away from Central Park and the city's premier shopping destinations, making it an ideal haven for those seeking both relaxation and the vibrancy of the city.

The Warren Street Hotel – Whimsical Elegance in Tribeca

Located in the heart of Tribeca, The Warren Street Hotel offers a delightful blend of British charm and contemporary design. Each of its 69 rooms is uniquely decorated with bold colors, eclectic furnishings, and floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the spaces with natural light. Some rooms feature private terraces, providing a tranquil escape amidst the city's bustle.

Guests can unwind in the hotel's cozy drawing room or savor seasonal dishes at the on-site restaurant, which boasts a vibrant atmosphere and artful decor. With its prime location, The Warren Street Hotel serves as a perfect base for exploring Tribeca's art galleries, boutiques, and culinary scene.

1 Hotel Brooklyn Bridge – Sustainable Luxury with Iconic Views

For those seeking eco-conscious luxury, 1 Hotel Brooklyn Bridge delivers an unparalleled experience. Located in Brooklyn Bridge Park, this hotel boasts panoramic views of the Manhattan skyline and the East River.

Its design emphasizes sustainability, featuring reclaimed materials, living plant walls, and organic linens. Rooms are adorned with natural elements, and some even offer hammocks for guests to relax while taking in the scenery. Popular attractions in New York

Three must-visit attractions in New York City that promise unforgettable experiences

Central Park – The Urban Oasis of New York

Spanning 843 acres in the heart of Manhattan, Central Park is more than just a park—it's the soul of New York City. Designed by Frederick Law Olmsted and Calvert Vaux in the 19th century, this green haven offers a serene escape from the city's hustle. Whether you're rowing on the lake, exploring the Ramble's winding paths, or enjoying a picnic on Sheep Meadow, there's something for everyone. The park is also home to the Central Park Zoo, the iconic Bethesda Terrace, and the Delacorte Theater, which hosts the beloved Shakespeare in the Park performances. With over 42 million visitors annually, Central Park is a testament to the city's dedication to preserving natural beauty amidst urban development.

The High Line – A Walk Above the City

Elevate your New York experience with a stroll along the High Line, a 1.45-mile-long elevated park built on a former railroad track. This innovative greenway offers panoramic views of the city and the Hudson River, complemented by beautifully landscaped gardens and art installations. As you walk, you'll pass through vibrant neighborhoods like Chelsea and the Meatpacking District, with easy access to attractions such as the Whitney Museum of American Art. The High Line is not just a park; it's a living, breathing example of urban revitalization and creativity.

Times Square – The Crossroads of the World

Experience the electric energy of Times Square, where the city's pulse is most palpable. Known for its dazzling billboards, Broadway theaters, and bustling crowds, Times Square is the epitome of New York's vibrant spirit. Whether you're catching a world-class performance, indulging in diverse culinary delights, or simply soaking in the ambiance, there's never a dull moment here. It's also the iconic site of the annual New Year's Eve ball drop, drawing millions of viewers worldwide.
